My Approach

I believe the most effective systems balance the needs of the people using them, the operational goals of the organization, and the meaningful data needed to measure impact.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Human-Centered Design

Drawing on my Anthropology background, I design systems around real human behavior rather than assumptions. My goal is to create experiences that are intuitive, accessible, and effective for participants and staff alike.

⚙️ Operational Excellence

I identify inefficiencies and create practical workflows that improve consistency, reduce friction, and support organizational success. Good systems should make work easier, not harder.

📊 Meaningful Data

I build processes that capture the information organizations need for reporting, evaluation, funding, and decision-making without creating unnecessary burden for users.
